---
name: Private visitor analytics
description: BeePod's privacy boundary for website traffic reporting.
---

BeePod visitor reporting must stay first-party and anonymous: do not add Google Analytics, advertising pixels, external tracking accounts, IP persistence, cross-device profiles, or fingerprinting without an explicit owner decision.

**Why:** The owner requested visitor analytics without Google tracking code and needs a private way to monitor launch interest.

**How to apply:** Keep visitor reporting aggregate-only in the owner dashboard. Any future tracking change should preserve the no-personal-profile boundary and avoid exposing visitor data alongside pre-order contacts.
