---
name: Pre-order lead operations
description: Scope boundary for BeePod launch sign-ups and the owner dashboard.
---

Treat BeePod pre-orders as launch-interest registrations, not payments, inventory reservations, or fulfillment orders. The dashboard is intentionally a single-owner operational surface rather than multi-user administration.

**Why:** BeePod is in a launch-stage lead-collection phase; payment, shipping, CRM automation, and team administration would add operational commitments that are not part of the current product promise.

**How to apply:** Keep public collection focused on contact details and launch interest. Add payment, fulfillment, outreach automation, exports, or staff access only as explicitly scoped follow-up work.
